How to read these figures. The 229 jurisdictions comprise 193 UN member states, two observer states (Palestine and the Holy See, represented here by Vatican City), two states in free association (Niue and the Cook Islands), Taiwan, and 31 non-sovereign territories. Svalbard and Jan Mayen share a statistical record but have different legal frameworks. A documented record does not establish that a specific light-pollution law exists or that its current legal force has been conclusively verified.

04 / INTERNATIONAL LAW

Light as energy. The reach of law.

The 1979 Geneva Convention, the International Law Commission, EU nature restoration and green procurement criteria. Interpretative relevance alone does not establish an enforceable offence.

↗ Legal analysis

Scope of the Convention: for transboundary air pollution under Article 1(b), the requirements concerning origin, effects in another state and distance must be met. The Convention does not establish universal CCT, ULOR or blue-light limits.

08 / METHODOLOGY AND LIMITATIONS

An evidence-based atlas.

This edition combines the available report on the latest expansion of 14 countries with a documented reconstruction of the remaining jurisdictions. Full reports from the earlier phases were unavailable for direct incorporation.

↗ Read master report
01

Categories are not rankings.

A country with guidelines may be innovative, while one with a national law may lack verifiable limits.

02

Jurisdiction before flag.

A regional law, a municipal rule and a national standard are not equivalent. Scope and exceptions are specified where identified.

03

Care with commencement and figures.

Transitional provisions, drafts and mandates to legislate are explicitly identified. Legal opinions require consulting the currently applicable official text.

04

Reusable data.

Source JSON, seis CSV, fichas Markdown, informe maestro y mapa local, incluidos en la distribución estática.
